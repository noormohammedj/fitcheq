How to start

  1. Open fitcheq.inraasolution.com on your phone. Add it to your home screen if you like.
  2. Tap Owner, Trainer or Member under “Trying the Prozone trial?” The mobile number and password fill in by themselves.
  3. Tap Sign in. To switch roles, use Sign out and pick another login.

Sample logins

Everyone uses a mobile number and a password. These sample logins are shared for the trial, so their passwords can’t be changed.

The 5 members you see (Arjun, Meera, Sameer, Divya and Rahul) are sample people with two weeks of history. To try it with your real members, sign in as the owner and use Add member. The app creates their login and gives you a WhatsApp message to send them.

What to try this week

As the owner
  • Check Business: fees collected, overdue, renewals, members at risk
  • See Trainer performance: who follows up with their clients
  • Add, edit or remove a trainer or member
  • Set your fee plans and opening hours
  • Add a real member on a fee plan and record their payment
  • Update facilities; send a payment reminder
As a trainer
  • Open a member and set their meals and workout for a day
  • Copy that day to the next 3 days, then change one
  • See who hasn’t come and send a WhatsApp reminder
  • See every client’s status in Client performance, and your own follow-up score
As a member
  • See today’s meals and workout set by your trainer
  • Tick a meal as had or skipped; mark the workout done or skipped
  • See your current, ideal and target weight
  • Check this week and this month; gym hours and fee plans
